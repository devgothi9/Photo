Problem

Checkout lines are the slowest part of shopping.

Solution

Scan on your phone. Cameras verify. Associates resolve mismatches.

Status

39 screens designed. Computer-vision pipeline in progress.

Design principle
Cameras never charge.

Shoppers pay only for what they scan. A mismatch becomes a quick associate check, never an accusation.

Key decisions

Design the edge cases first.

01

Failure states

Barcode not found, payment failed, produce by weight each get a screen.

02

Evidence over alerts

Every flag shows a 12-second clip and a timeline. "Incorrect detection" is one tap.

03

Real-time cart

Totals and stock update as items go in or out.
