SafeSpace

Anonymous mental-wellness platform with an AI journal coach. Built and shipped solo.

Founder2025 – Present
SSafeSpace
Problem

Wellness apps ask who you are before you feel safe.

Build

Anonymous chat, an AI journal coach and weekly mood reports.

Result

Live on web and PWA. Android in Play Store review.

Key decisions

Anonymity is the feature.

01

Privacy in the database

Supabase Row Level Security, so no client can read another user's data.

02

AI that coaches

An LLM journal coach and automated mood reports. No diagnoses.

03

Fair pricing

Stripe freemium in 8 currencies, with trials and webhooks.
