Problem

Photo portfolios are often slow galleries or templates that crop and clutter the work.

Approach

Treat the site like a product: one job, which is showing the photo well on any screen.

Result

A live, image-first portfolio on Vercel that I shoot, curate, design and ship myself.

01 — Why

Photography taught me to frame before I design.

Choosing what to leave out of a shot is the same skill as choosing what to leave out of a screen. This site is where both habits meet.

02 — Principles

Less interface, more image.

01

The frame is the hero

No UI competes with the photo. Navigation stays quiet so the image carries the page.

02

Fast on a phone

Most people open a portfolio link on mobile, so the layout is mobile-first and deployed on Vercel.

03

Curation over volume

Fewer, stronger frames. A tight edit says more than an endless scroll.
